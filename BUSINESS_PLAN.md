# SmartChantierAI — Business Plan (Executive Summary)

## Vision

SmartChantier AI is the **operating system for French construction sites** — unifying project tracking, AI-assisted procurement, quotations, technical sheets, OCR documents, and field reporting in one multilingual platform (FR / AR / EN).

## Problem

- Fragmented tools (Excel, WhatsApp, supplier portals)
- Manual devis and purchase orders with errors
- No real-time visibility for clients and directors
- Language barriers on multicultural crews (FR + AR)

## Solution

SaaS platform with:

- **AI Purchase Assistant** — real web search (Tavily) + supplier comparison
- **Devis & BC PDF** — professional Unicode exports
- **Plan OCR pipeline** — rooms, materials, colored zones
- **Role-based access** — admin, chef de projet, chef de chantier, client
- **Supabase backend** — scalable multi-tenant data

## Market

- **TAM:** ~650k construction companies in France (BTP)
- **SAM:** 45k companies > 10 employees using digital tools
- **SOM (Year 3):** 800 paying accounts @ €89–249/mo

## Revenue model

| Plan | Price/mo | Target |
